FAQ

Questions students usually ask.

Is UniHub free for students?

Yes. The student account and core platform are designed to be free.

Which universities can join?

UniHub includes IT-related faculties and study programmes from public and private universities across North Macedonia, with an “Other” option so no accredited IT student is excluded.

Can students upload notes and exams?

Yes. Logged-in students can contribute resources, while moderation and reporting keep content organized.
