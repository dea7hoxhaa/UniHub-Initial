PRIVACY

Privacy at UniHub

UniHub is a student project designed to demonstrate a connected platform for university students.

Information you provide

The demo interface may ask for your name, university, faculty, study programme, study year and interests to personalize the experience.

Cookies and browser storage

UniHub uses essential browser storage to keep you signed in, remember onboarding and profile details, save small preferences such as theme, language and the last viewed tab, and store your cookie consent choice. These items help the app work and personalize the demo experience.

The current version does not use advertising cookies or marketing analytics. If non-essential storage, analytics or advertising tools are added later, they should only run after you accept them.

Contact form

The current contact form is a presentation feature and is not yet connected to a production messaging backend.

Future backend integration

The project is planned to use Supabase for authentication and database functionality. This privacy information should be reviewed when that integration is finalized.

Analytics and advertising

The current presentation version does not include advertising or marketing analytics.
