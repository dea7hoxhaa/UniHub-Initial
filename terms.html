UniHub Terms of Use

UniHub is currently an academic project and demonstration platform.

Student community use

Users should use UniHub respectfully and should not publish misleading, harmful, abusive or intentionally false information.

Study resources and previous exams

Students should only upload materials they are allowed to share and should organize them under the correct university, faculty or subject.

Internships, events and announcements

Users should verify important details with the original organization or source.

Teammates, roommates and community posts

Users should communicate responsibly and avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal information publicly.

Project status

Some actions are demonstrated through frontend interactions and may not yet represent a fully deployed production service.
